From One “Good Morning”

to Forever ❤️

🌹 Rose Day — First Sight

Everything started on May 20, 2024 at 10:05 AM. A normal office morning… And then she walked in. Smiling. Wishing everyone good morning. That moment quietly planted the first rose of love in my heart.

💍 Propose Day — Finding Courage

For weeks we sat in the same office, yet I couldn’t talk properly. So I tried something old-school… leaving my photo inside her book. Somehow, it worked. We exchanged numbers — and everything changed.

Will you be mine forever? 💍

🍫 Chocolate Day — Sweet Conversations

“Hi… Hello… How was your day?” Soon we were talking till 3–4 AM. Sleep didn’t matter. Talking to her felt sweeter than chocolate.

🧸 Teddy Day — Comfort & Friendship

Lunch breaks became park walks. Side by side. Laughing. Slowly our hands touched. She became my comfort. My safe place.

🤝 Promise Day — The Journey Together

Our Vaishno Devi trip. Train laughs. Ice creams. A 13 km trek. Somewhere during that climb, we made a silent promise — no matter how hard life gets, we’ll walk together.

🤗 Hug Day — Closeness

After the trip everything felt deeper. Even silence felt warm. Just sitting beside her felt like home.

💋 Kiss Day — First Confession

In the park, heart racing, I kissed her gently on the forehead and finally said — “I love you.”

❤️ Valentine’s Day — Forever

From one simple “Good Morning”… to late night calls, to holding hands, to love. You became my home. Forever.

Will you stay with me forever? 💌
